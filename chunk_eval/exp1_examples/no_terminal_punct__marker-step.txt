- AB = 19.5, BC = 14.15

\[
\text{Perimeter of Rectangle ABCD} = 2 \times 19.5 + 14.15
\]
\[
\text{Length of semi-circle arc} = \frac{\pi \times 14.15}{2}
\]

Using \(\pi = 3.14\):

\[
\text{Length of semi-circle arc} = \frac{3.14 \times 14.15}{2} = \frac{44.43}{2} \approx 22.21
\]

Combine these lengths to find the total perimeter:

\[
\text{Perimeter of ABCD} = 39.0 + 14.15 + 22.21 = 75.36
